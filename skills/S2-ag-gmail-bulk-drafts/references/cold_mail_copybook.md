# Cold Mail Copybook

> 用途：集中管理 Cold Mail 的模板、variant、固定语气和拼装规则。
> 这是一份策略资产库，不是执行 SOP。

## 1. Core Purpose

- 用于首封陌生邮件触达
- 目标是建立合作意向，并索取 media kit / channel links / rates
- 风格应专业、克制、可复制，不像 mass blast
- 首封邮件的核心不是成交，而是建立一个可信、可推进的商务入口

## 2. Messaging Principles

- 先明确身份，再建立可信度
- `Hook` 必须回答“为什么是你”
- 主体结构固定，但 CTA 强度应按 variant 区分
- 首封邮件优先索要资料，不直接进入过深谈判
- 如果要更直接索价，也仍应保持职业节奏，而不是压迫式推进

## 3. Fixed Blocks

### 3.1 Sender Intro Pool

#### S1 Standard Intro

- `I'm Steve from CoreStar Agency.`
- 适用：
  - 通用默认版

#### S2 Agency Positioning Intro

- `I'm Steve from CoreStar Agency, where we help AI and tech brands work with creators whose audiences are a real fit.`
- 适用：
  - 想更清楚说明 agency 角色

#### S3 Light Commercial Intro

- `I'm Steve from CoreStar Agency, and I'm reaching out regarding upcoming AI creator partnerships.`
- 适用：
  - 直接进入商务语境

### 3.2 Credibility Block Pool

#### C1 Standard Credibility

- `Our team has a strong track record in the AI and tech ecosystem.`
- `We have delivered high-impact campaigns for leading brands including Skywork AI, Kling AI, Seedance, Alibaba, and TikTok.`

#### C2 Fit-First Credibility

- `We work closely with AI and tech brands that care about audience fit and content quality, not just broad reach.`

#### C3 Short Credibility

- `We support AI and tech brands on creator partnerships across campaign sourcing, fit assessment, and execution.`

### 3.3 Ask Block Pool

#### A1 Full Intake Ask

- `Links to all your active social channels`
- `Your latest Media Kit`
- `Current rates for all channels`

#### A2 Media Kit First

- `Your latest Media Kit`
- `Links to your active channels`

#### A3 Rates First

- `Your current rates for dedicated videos and integrations`
- `Any up-to-date media kit or audience overview if available`

### 3.4 Signature

```text
Best regards,
Steve
CoreStar Agency
```

## 4. Greeting Rule

- 真人名：直接叫名字
- 组织 / 品牌 / 频道：加 `Team`
- 不确定时，宁可保守，也不要叫错人名
- `Subject` 使用清洗后的主体，不带末尾 `Team`

## 5. Subject Patterns

### P1 Standard Collaboration

- `Paid Collaboration: {Display_Name} x CoreStar Agency`

### P2 Campaign-Oriented

- `Paid Collaboration: Q2 AI Projects x {Display_Name} - CoreStar Agency`

### P3 Neutral Partnership

- `Creator Partnership Inquiry: {Display_Name} x CoreStar Agency`

### P4 Fit-First

- `Potential AI Campaign Fit: {Display_Name} x CoreStar Agency`

规则：
- 清晰、正式、可商务接受
- 不堆叠过多形容词
- 不写成明显营销标题党
- 不要用过度兴奋语气

## 6. Variant Matrix

### A1_media_kit_soft

- 用途：首次陌生触达，先要 media kit / rates
- 开场建议：
  - `S1/S2 + C1/C2`
- CTA：
  - 先要资料，不直接谈预算
- 适合：
  - 证据中等
  - 先建合作入口
- 不适合：
  - 你已经明确只差 rate card

### A2_rate_card_direct

- 用途：已明确要报价
- 开场建议：
  - `S1/S3 + C2/C3`
- CTA：
  - 直接索要 current rates / rate card
- 适合：
  - 已有较强 fit
  - 内部当前核心目标就是定价判断

### B1_creator_specific_soft

- 用途：内容匹配证据强，希望强调 fit
- 开场建议：
  - `S2 + C2`
- CTA：
  - 柔和推进，先资料后 brief
- 适合：
  - founder / educator / operator 这类内容逻辑清晰账号

### B2_creator_specific_direct

- 用途：内容 fit 明确，且本轮就是要报价
- 开场建议：
  - `S2/S3 + C2/C3`
- CTA：
  - 强调匹配 + 直接询价
- 不适合：
  - 初次接触且资料证据弱

## 7. CTA Strategy

### Soft CTA

适用：
- 首次陌生触达
- 目标是拿资料

典型表达：
- `Could you please send over your latest media kit, active channel links, and current rates?`
- `I’d love to keep your profile in consideration for upcoming AI campaigns if you’re open to sharing your current media kit and rates.`

### Direct CTA

适用：
- 已有较强 fit
- 内部当轮重点是定价

典型表达：
- `Could you share your current rates for dedicated videos and integrations?`
- `If available, I’d also appreciate your latest media kit or any recent audience overview.`

## 8. Assembly Rules

固定结构：

```text
Hi {Mail1_Greeting_Name},

{Sender Intro}

{Credibility Block}

{Mail1_Hook}

{Variant Body}

{CTA Block}

{Signature}
```

规则：
- `Mail1_Hook` 必须来自行内证据
- `Variant Body` 决定语气和推进强度
- `CTA Block` 决定“先要资料”还是“先要 rates”
- 主结构固定，不整封自由重写
- 写回 CSV 时必须保留真实换行

## 9. Do / Don't

Do:
- 说清为什么找这个 creator
- 保持专业商务语气
- 优先建立合作入口，而不是一步谈到底
- 让邮件像真人商务触达，而不是群发模板

Don't:
- 空泛夸赞
- 叫错名字
- 首封就压预算、谈执行细节太深
- 使用像 spam 的夸张表达
- 把所有要求一次性堆满到同一段里

## 10. Risk Language

谨慎使用：
- `exclusive opportunity`
- `limited spots`
- `urgent`
- `ASAP`
- 过度 salesy 的夸张形容词

原因：
- 首封陌生邮件太容易显得像 mass blast
- 也会削弱 agency 的专业姿态

## 11. Good Examples

### Example A1

```text
Hi {Mail1_Greeting_Name},

I'm Steve from CoreStar Agency.

Our team has a strong track record in the AI and tech ecosystem.
We have delivered high-impact campaigns for leading brands including Skywork AI, Kling AI, Seedance, Alibaba, and TikTok.

{Mail1_Hook}

We are currently building out our shortlist for upcoming AI creator campaigns, and I would love to keep your profile in consideration where there is a strong audience fit.

Could you please send over your latest media kit, links to your active channels, and your current rates for dedicated videos or integrations?

Best regards,
Steve
CoreStar Agency
```

### Example A2

```text
Hi {Mail1_Greeting_Name},

I'm Steve from CoreStar Agency, and I'm reaching out regarding upcoming AI creator partnerships.

We work closely with AI and tech brands that care about audience fit and content quality, not just broad reach.

{Mail1_Hook}

Given the alignment here, I would love to understand your current rates for dedicated videos and integrations.

If available, I’d also appreciate your latest media kit or any recent audience overview.

Best regards,
Steve
CoreStar Agency
```

### Example B1

```text
Hi {Mail1_Greeting_Name},

I'm Steve from CoreStar Agency, where we help AI and tech brands work with creators whose audiences are a real fit.

We work closely with AI and tech brands that care about audience fit and content quality, not just broad reach.

{Mail1_Hook}

That is why I thought your profile would make sense to keep on hand for upcoming AI partnerships where fit matters more than broad distribution alone.

If you’re open, I’d love to receive your latest media kit, active channel links, and current collaboration rates.

Best regards,
Steve
CoreStar Agency
```

## 12. Bad Example

```text
Hi creator,

We love your amazing content and think you would be perfect for our exciting AI campaign. Please send your rates, all channel links, audience stats, and availability ASAP because this is a limited opportunity.
```

问题：
- 称呼错误且泛化
- 夸赞空洞
- 像 mass blast
- CTA 过重
- 压迫感太强
